# Event synthesize

Read `../../../AGENTS.md`. Write event knowledge under `docs/`, following the
existing `docs/days/` and `docs/reference/` structure. This skill does not grant
publication rights or authorize fetching additional days.

## Inputs and coverage

Inventory reviewed chapters, source IDs/time ranges, attribution ledger,
transcription coverage, and supporting demonstrations/artifacts. Declare the
available evidence and its limitations before drawing conclusions. Missing day
material is pending, not a negative finding or proof that nothing happened.
Days two and three stay explicitly pending until acquired and reviewed; never
invent their discussions, outcomes, lessons, or completion percentages.

## Produce evidence-owned knowledge

- **Decisions:** Identify the decision, who actually made/accepted it, timestamp,
  rationale, alternatives if stated, constraints, and status. Distinguish a
  proposal, bot suggestion, or audience recommendation from an accepted decision.
  Do not create a governance ratification or acceptance on a speaker's behalf.
- **Progress:** Separate planned, attempted, demonstrated, independently verified,
  blocked, and pending work. A live demo or speaker claim has a different
  evidentiary weight from a verified artifact/test. Give numerator/denominator
  and scope for any completion metric; do not infer project completion from
  transcription coverage.
- **Lessons:** Separate direct observations, participant opinions, and analyst
  hypotheses. Link the evidence and note counterexamples/limits. A single
  demonstration does not establish a universal product or engineering claim.
- **Open work:** Record unanswered questions, contradictory accounts, missing
  sources, and validation needed. Name an owner/date only if explicitly stated;
  otherwise use unknown/unassigned rather than guessing.

For each consequential item retain: source day and ID, source-relative interval,
chapter/transcript reference, speaker/entity and attribution confidence,
evidence type, concise nonverbatim claim, supporting or conflicting evidence,
and current status. Follow the raw source if an edited chapter conflicts with
it. Preserve prior synthesis history rather than quietly rewriting conclusions.

## Cross-day reconciliation and review

1. Deduplicate recurring themes without collapsing distinct occurrences. Link
   each occurrence and date/day; distinguish a reiterated promise from a later
   demonstrated result.
2. Keep human Steve/Jenny and bot Steve/Jenny separate as the attribution ledger
   requires. Preserve speaker-versus-output-author distinctions when a human
   reads generated advice. Do not credit bots with independently observed work.
3. When later evidence changes an assessment, record what changed, the new
   evidence, and which earlier uncertainty is resolved. Pending days cannot
   supply corroboration.
4. Review substantive claims against their cited intervals and classify all
   uncertain attribution and nonverbatim synthesis explicitly. Do a privacy,
   copyright, and authorization review before any publication handoff.

## Acceptance

Return the updated evidence-linked synthesis, coverage by day, decision/progress
status, supported lessons, and unresolved questions. Every claimed outcome must
have reviewed support. Clearly distinguish what was written from what was
verified, and what is still pending. No auto-stage, commit, push, or publishing.
