## Reading and attribution conventions

Sections preserve grouped exchanges rather than invented diarized turns. The
speaker/type labels and surrounding prose distinguish requests, advice, human
narration, claimed bot output and reported results. Unless a narrower addressee
is stated, host discussion is to cohosts/viewers and workshop explanation is to
the audience. Human narrators remain separate from claimed bot authors.

The [entity ledger](../entities.md) owns stable IDs: D2-H01/H02/H03 are the host
candidates; D2-H04 is the early workshop presenter; D2-H05/H06 the coffee/creator
guests; D2-H07/H08 the sales presenters; D2-H09 the Berman guest; D2-H10 the
customer-video speaker; D2-H11 the student; D2-H12/H13 the SDR/support presenters.
Unresolved audience and mixed speakers use D2-HU, a category rather than a person.

All substantive recoverable exchanges were targeted for retention. Filler, waits,
repeated setup explanations and promotional repetition are condensed; unrecovered
speech, music, privacy exclusions and screen-only gaps are explicitly logged.
Interval checks cannot prove semantic completeness; audio review remains a gate.
